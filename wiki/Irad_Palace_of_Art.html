    Irad Palace of Art

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Irad Palace of Art (Iradi: Irad Körköm Saraiy) is an art gallery and art preservation foundation headquartered in Iyık Janyl. It is generally regarded as the country's leading gallery.[1]

    History and collections

    The foundation was established in 1996, during the territorial period, and occupies a former Soviet district house of culture near the cathedral. Its collections include:

    • Sogdian terracottas and Silk Road ceramics;
    • icons, church silver and manuscript bindings that families hid during the Soviet period and later gave to the foundation;
    • saddlery, felt and embroidery of the 19th and 20th centuries;
    • the largest collection of the work of Yusup Ayyubov, including The White Rider Shows the Way (1913);
    • Soviet-era Iradi painting;
    • contemporary painting, photography and video.[1]

    Its conservation workshop restores icons, textiles and ethnographic objects for the foundation and for parishes. Since the 2010s it has run an annual exhibition and grant program for young artists, many associated with the Iradi language revival. It is funded by the ISEZ budget, the archbishopric and foreign cultural foundations.[1][2]
