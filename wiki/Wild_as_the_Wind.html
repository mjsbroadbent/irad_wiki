    Wild as the Wind

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    "Wild as the Wind" is a country ballad written and recorded by Jesse Rourke for the 1993 charity compilation Swift Hooves.[1]

    Production

    The song is in the dark, reverb-soaked style of late-1980s country: a slow tempo, a clean electric guitar with heavy tremolo, soft bass and light brushed drums, and Rourke's close-miked baritone breaking into falsetto on the hook.[1]

    Themes

    A young man "born in Irad's dust" hears the smugglers' horses on the ridge every night and is drawn to a life he knows will ruin him. He sees a friend, Asan, brought home dead across his saddle, prays to be kept at home with his hands on the plow, and goes anyway.[1]
