    My Home My Heart My Soul

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    "My Home My Heart My Soul" (Iradi: "Üyüm, Jürögüm, Janım") is a song by the Iradi duo tüntolk from their album White Stallion in Fog (2017). It is often called the strangest song in their catalogue, and it is the song most associated with the 2023 Student Protests in Talas.[1][2]

    Music and lyrics

    The song is built on a deep, continuous hum, against which Mira Temirova screams the lyrics with brutal, distorted force. Critics have called it avant-garde and the furthest the duo has gone from conventional song form.[1] The text is short and plain. Its refrain, "Oh Irad, my home, my heart, my soul", is taken from the refrain of the national anthem, "The Spirit of Irad", and is repeated after each line, with verses about the land "where the wind whispers secrets, which only brave hearts know" and "where the ancient legends live on even now", ending "in the land of Irad, where I want to stay". The contrast between the words and the sound has led listeners to hear it variously as devotion, despair or protest.[2]

    In the 2023 protests

    During the student protests in Talas in October 2023, marchers chanted the song's refrain, "Ooo İrad menin üyüm, jürögüm, janım". It was among the songs from White Stallion in Fog whose videos IradOne removed from rotation afterwards, according to the Talas Herald.[3] Commentators noted that the broadcaster had stopped showing a song whose refrain comes from the national anthem, which IradOne plays at the start and close of every broadcasting day. The duo said only that their songs "belong to whoever sings them".[3]
