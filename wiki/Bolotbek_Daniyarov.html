    Bolotbek Daniyarov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Bolotbek Daniyarov (born 1964) is an Iradi journalist who has been managing editor of the Irad Times, an independent English-language weekly published in Talas, since 2001.[1]

    The Irad Times was founded in 1996, during the territorial period. In December 2006 and January 2007, under Daniyarov's editorship, it published internal documents on the planned sale of state oil, grain and mining assets by the government of the Democratic Republic of Irad. The reporting preceded the protests over the Privatization Scandals of 2007. On February 26, 2007, the paper printed in a special edition the partition drafts that Erlan Toktarov had released the day before (see Bill of Sale (2007)).[1] Since 2007 the paper has operated under the media rules of the Irad Special Economic Zone. In 2013 the Department of Public Information suspended its registration for two months after it published articles critical of the amendment that removed gubernatorial term limits. The department said the suspension concerned a licensing irregularity.[2]
