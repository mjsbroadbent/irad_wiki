    Bazar-i Kalan

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Bazar-i Kalan ("great bazaar") is the covered central market of Talas and the market quarter around it, on the eastern edge of the Old Talas Citadel Quarter. A bazaar has stood on the site since at least the Kokand period, when it grew up beside the rebuilt citadel; the present covered hall was built in the 1950s.[1] The market is open daily and sells fruit and vegetables, spices, dried fruit, beef and mutton, dairy products, felt rugs, clothing and electronics. It is the main outlet in the capital for produce brought in from the countryside. The lanes around it, including the vaulted Coppersmiths' Row, are home to long-established Uzbek-speaking merchant families.[1][2] In the 2000s its electronics stalls were known for cheap pirated software discs, which the nearby software company Kuble Soft, maker of a 1995 trading game named after the bazaar, blamed for its closure in 2007.
