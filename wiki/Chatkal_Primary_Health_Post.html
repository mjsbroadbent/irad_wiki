    Chatkal Primary Health Post

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Chatkal Primary Health Post is a rural clinic in Sary-Bulak District that serves the families of workers at the Chatkal antimony smelter and nearby villages. Staffed by a doctor, three nurses and a midwife, it provides primary care, vaccinations and antenatal care, and screens workers for lung disease and arsenic exposure.[1] It is funded by the ISEZ health authorities and by the smelting company.[1]
