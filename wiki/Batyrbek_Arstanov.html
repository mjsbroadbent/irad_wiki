    Batyrbek Arstanov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Batyrbek Arstanov (1921 – 2001) was an Iradi cavalryman and horse breeder. He served in a cavalry division raised in Central Asia during the Second World War and fought in the Caucasus and Ukraine.[1] After the war he worked as a horse breeder at the state stud near Talas. In 1995, aged 74, he rode at the head of the column of war veterans at the Irad Day parade in Talas, which marked the 50th anniversary of the end of the war. He was the father of the breeder Keneshbek Arstanov.[2]
